What's inside

Everything in one place — you pick the pieces

MyWholeHQ is built from modules. Turn on what your business needs; your menus show only what's on. Nothing is forced, and turning a module off never deletes your data.

Client database

Every client, job, photo, and note in one searchable record — find anyone by name, service, date, or open invoice.

Smart scheduling

Per-person availability, service areas, buffer times, and drive-time rules — so the calendar respects the road, not just the clock.

Invoicing & online payments

Invoices clients can pay by card or bank transfer through Stripe. The money goes straight to your own account, and card numbers never touch the app.

Texting, built in

Two-way texting with clients and subs from your business number, right inside the app — plus bulk texts and emails when a whole list needs to hear from you. Every conversation stays on the record it belongs to.

Subcontractors & bid requests

Your subs in one directory, with license and insurance paperwork tracked. Send a bid request to the whole list — each sub gets their own private link, bids come back to one place, and you award the job from there.

Online booking & lead forms

Share your booking page and lead form anywhere — your website, your socials, a QR code on the truck. Every request lands in the app, ready to work.

Follow-ups & reviews

After the job, MyWholeHQ keeps the follow-up in front of you — the thank-you, the feedback ask, your review links — so staying in touch never depends on memory.

Money ledger

Every dollar traced to where it came from: lead source, referrals and referral credits, every discount given, and what's past due — all in one ledger.

Expenses, receipts & mileage

Log expenses, snap receipt photos, and track mileage as you go — no shoebox of paper at tax time.

Retail & inventory

Products and SKUs, stock across locations, low-stock alerts, purchase orders, quick sales, and returns.

People & hiring

Applicant tracking with interview notes, and guided onboarding so new hires complete their paperwork and training in one flow.

SOPs & document Vault

Your standard procedures in one library your team can actually follow, plus a protected Vault for licenses, contracts, and financial documents.

Trust & security

Built security-first, from day one

Stripe holds the card data

Payments run through Stripe. Card and bank numbers are never stored in MyWholeHQ — not on our servers, not in the app.

Your data stays yours

Each business's data is kept separate, with role-based access and a full activity log — and it's backed up automatically every day. Your client list is your business's asset — not ours to sell or share.

Your accountant can look — not touch

A read-only Accountant role lets your CPA pull reports and a year-end tax packet directly, without ever being able to change your books.

Sign up, confirm your email with the code we send, and set up your business — your 14 days start right then. Every module is included and no card is asked for. If you never subscribe, you're never charged.

What happens when my trial ends?

Subscribe from inside the app to keep going — $29/month per business on the Founding plan. Your data stays put either way; nothing is deleted because a trial ran out.

Is it really unlimited users?

Yes. The price is per business, not per person — your whole crew, office help included, at no extra charge.

How do my customers pay me?

Your invoices can be paid by card or bank transfer through Stripe, and the money goes straight to your own account. Your MyWholeHQ subscription is separate, and card numbers never touch the app.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel from inside the app whenever you like. No phone call, no retention script, and your data stays available as our Terms describe.

What if I need help setting up?

Email us and a real person answers — usually within one business day. Founding businesses get personal setup help while we grow.
