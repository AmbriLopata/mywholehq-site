Privacy Policy

Your data, in plain language.

What MyWholeHQ collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Who we are

MyWholeHQ is currently operated by New Life General Contracting LLC. This policy covers the MyWholeHQ app and website. It works together with our Terms of Service.

Two kinds of data

  • Account data — about you and your business: your name and email, your business's name and settings, and the records of how the account is used. For this data, we are the controller.
  • Your business's client data — the information you store about YOUR clients (names, contact details, addresses, job and payment records, photos, documents). That data belongs to your business. For it, we are a processor: we store and handle it only on your instructions, so your business can run. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for our own marketing.

What we collect, and why

  • Sign-in and account details (name, email, a hashed password — never the password itself): to create and secure your account, send sign-in and password-reset messages, and support you.
  • Business records you enter (clients, jobs, estimates, invoices, expenses, mileage, appointments, photos and files you upload): to run the service you asked for. This is the product.
  • Payment metadata (amounts, dates, methods, invoice links, refund status): to show accurate invoice and payment records. Card and bank details are handled by Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • Connection tokens, only if you choose to connect a service (for example Gmail): so the connected feature can work. Tokens are stored encrypted at rest when the platform's encryption key is configured, and are never shown to other businesses.
  • Technical records (error logs and basic request information): to find and fix problems and keep the service secure. Error logs are kept without request bodies, headers, or credentials.

Cookies and sessions

MyWholeHQ uses a small number of cookies — essentially a session cookie that keeps you signed in, plus the preferences your browser stores for the app. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not run third-party trackers or analytics beacons in the app. Signing out ends the session.

Who data is shared with (sub-processors)

We share only what a provider needs to do its job:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Receives payment details directly from your clients and payment metadata tied to your invoices. Stripe also processes your own MyWholeHQ plan subscription, if you have one: it receives your payment details at checkout and sends us the subscription status and payment metadata for your plan record.
  • Resend — email delivery. Receives the emails the app sends for you (invites, reminders, documents) and their recipients.
  • Twilio — text messaging. If a business uses the app's texting feature, phone numbers and message content pass through Twilio so texts can be sent to and received from that business's clients on the business's behalf.
  • Google — only if you connect it: Gmail (email history for a business's own records) and Google Places (business/place lookups). Nothing is shared with Google for businesses that never connect it.
  • Render — hosting. The app and its database run on Render's infrastructure, so all stored data physically lives there, under our account.

Other than these providers, we do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if the law genuinely requires it, or to protect the service and other businesses from fraud or abuse.

Security

  • Passwords are stored as strong one-way hashes (Argon2id) — never in plain text, and never recoverable by us.
  • Every business's data is walled off: a signed-in user can only ever reach the businesses they belong to, and that is checked on the server for every request.
  • Stored connection secrets (such as Gmail tokens and webhook signing secrets) are encrypted at rest when the platform encryption key is configured.
  • Backups of the database and files are taken regularly so a failure does not mean lost businesses.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell affected account owners without unreasonable delay.

Your rights and choices

  • Export: download your whole business from inside the app at any time — records, files, and settings in one archive.
  • Deletion: delete your business from inside the app. Its records and files are removed from the live service right away.
  • Correction: most of your data is yours to edit in the app at any time. For account details you can't edit yourself, contact us.
  • Questions and requests about your data: email newlifegc.indy@gmail.com. If you are a client of a business that uses MyWholeHQ, contact that business first — they control their records; we will help them honor your request.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your MyWholeHQ account and the data associated with it, email newlifegc.indy@gmail.com from the email address on the account. We process deletion requests within 30 days. Deletion removes your account and the business data you stored in MyWholeHQ — clients, jobs, invoices, documents, and messages. Some records must be kept: records we are required to retain for legal, tax, or accounting obligations (for example, payment records held by Stripe), and encrypted backups, which are purged within 30 days.

Retention after deletion

When a business is deleted, its records and files are removed from the live service immediately. Routine backups are kept for a limited rotation and then overwritten, so deleted data ages out of backups on that cycle rather than living forever. A small platform error log (which contains no client records, bodies, or credentials) is retained on its own short rotation for operating the service.

Children

MyWholeHQ is a business tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service grows. We will change the "Last updated" date below and, for changes that matter, tell account owners in the app or by email before they take effect.

Contact
